Bars show outer-fold means and sample SD, not standard errors. Total wall time includes cache IO; feature time includes preprocessing, operator construction, WL, projections and quantiles. The observed Pareto frontier consists of methods for which no other method has accuracy at least as high and runtime at most as large, with one strict inequality. It ignores uncertainty and is not a model-selection rule. Speedup compares full eligible feature grids, not equal-work per-graph complexity. Temperature and degree-gap histograms use structures only. Each undirected edge is counted once. Paired differences use matching held-out folds. Authors-style outputs are separate and use the same style for every method in their figures.
